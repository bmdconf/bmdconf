Dear colleagues,

We are pleased to announce that the **6th International Bicycle & Motorcycle
Dynamics Conference (BMD 2027)** will take place from **9 to 11 June 2027** in
**Lyon, France**.

BMD brings together the international community of scientists, researchers and
engineers working in the broad field of bicycle and motorcycle dynamics and
control. The conference will feature a single-track scientific programme,
poster sessions, and dedicated opportunities for discussion and networking.

Further information, including the Call for Papers, conference website and
registration details, will be announced in the coming months.

We look forward to welcoming you to Lyon!

Best regards,

The BMD 2027 Organising Committee
