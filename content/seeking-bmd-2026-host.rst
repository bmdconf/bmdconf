==========================
Seeking Host for BMD 2026+
==========================

:date: 2026-01-28 10:10:00
:tags:
:category: news
:authors: Jason K. Moore

Unfortunately, the 2026 conference will not occur in Japan this year due to
unforeseen circumstances. The scientific committee is now seeking a host for
the next conference symposium. To try keep close to the three-year cycle, we
hope to arrange the next conference as soon as feasible. If you are interested
in being the next host or know someone who might be, please contact Jason K.
Moore at j.k.moore@tudelft.nl.
